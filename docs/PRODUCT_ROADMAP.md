## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
- **Skill:** Scalability → Load test monthly
- **Fix:** Database optimization, caching

### Business Risks
- **Skill:** Pro tier adoption slow → Offer annual discount
- **Fix:** Free tier features maintain, no paywall fear

### Market Risks
- **Skill:** Competitor copies model → Community loyalty
- **Fix:** Quality > quantity, MIT license = trust

### Team Risks
- **Skill:** Founder burnout → Hire early
- **Fix:** Series A funding by month 10
