# Implement Continuous Improvement Program

> Establish systematic approach to ongoing process improvement and optimization

## Quick Start
1. Review the requirements and scope
2. Gather necessary resources and tools
3. Follow the step-by-step instructions below

## When to Use
- Establish systematic approach to ongoing process improvement and optimization
- Part of Quality Assurance workflow
- Professional development and team training

## Step-by-Step
1. Define improvement objectives and metrics
2. Select improvement methodology (Lean, Six Sigma, Kaizen)
3. Form improvement team
4. Map current processes
5. Identify waste, bottlenecks, and inefficiencies

## Dependencies
- Kaizen boards
- Six Sigma tools
- Value stream mapping

## Best Practices
- ✅ Involve frontline employees
- ✅ Focus on quick wins first
- ✅ Use data for decision-making

## Validation
- Complete each step in sequence
- Verify deliverables meet requirements
- Document results and lessons learned

- Quality Assurance fundamentals
