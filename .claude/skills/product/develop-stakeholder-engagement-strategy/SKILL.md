# Develop Stakeholder Engagement Strategy

> Create a plan to identify, analyze, and engage stakeholders throughout the project

## Quick Start
1. Review the requirements and scope
2. Gather necessary resources and tools
3. Follow the step-by-step instructions below

## When to Use
- Create a plan to identify, analyze, and engage stakeholders throughout the project
- Part of Project Management workflow
- Professional development and team training

## Step-by-Step
1. Identify all project stakeholders
2. Analyze stakeholder interests and influence
3. Assess current engagement level
4. Define desired engagement level for each stakeholder
5. Develop engagement tactics and messages

## Dependencies
- Stakeholder Circle
- Miro
- Slack

## Best Practices
- ✅ Include stakeholders early and often
- ✅ Tailor communication to audience
- ✅ Address concerns transparently

## Validation
- Complete each step in sequence
- Verify deliverables meet requirements
- Document results and lessons learned
