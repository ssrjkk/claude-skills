# Lead Organizational Change Initiative

> Guide organization through significant change with communication and support

## Quick Start
1. Review the requirements and scope
2. Gather necessary resources and tools
3. Follow the step-by-step instructions below

## When to Use
- Guide organization through significant change with communication and support
- Part of Leadership workflow
- Professional development and team training

## Step-by-Step
1. Understand current state and desired future state
2. Create compelling vision for change
3. Identify stakeholders and build coalition
4. Develop detailed change management plan
5. Implement communication strategy

## Dependencies
- Change management frameworks
- Surveys
- Focus groups

## Best Practices
- ✅ Communicate early and often
- ✅ Involve affected stakeholders
- ✅ Address resistance with empathy

## Validation
- Complete each step in sequence
- Verify deliverables meet requirements
- Document results and lessons learned
