# Conduct Risk Assessment and Planning

> Identify, analyze, and develop response strategies for project risks

## Quick Start
1. Review the requirements and scope
2. Gather necessary resources and tools
3. Follow the step-by-step instructions below

## When to Use
- Identify, analyze, and develop response strategies for project risks
- Part of Project Management workflow
- Professional development and team training

## Step-by-Step
1. Conduct risk brainstorming session with team
2. Document identified risks with descriptions
3. Categorize risks by source and type
4. Assess probability and impact of each risk
5. Prioritize risks using risk matrix

## Dependencies
- Risk Radar
- Palisade @RISK
- Oracle Risk Management

## Best Practices
- ✅ Involve cross-functional team in identification
- ✅ Update risk register regularly
- ✅ Distinguish between risks and issues

## Validation
- Complete each step in sequence
- Verify deliverables meet requirements
- Document results and lessons learned
